ABOUT

HI, I’M PAU AYLLÓN!

I’m a 3D artist and a Programmer from Valencia, Spain. I studied a Digital Technology and Multimedia Degree at the Polytechnic University of Valencia.

My style is heavily inspired by movies like “Spider-Man: Into the Spiderverse” and “Arcane”. I'm in love with the world of animation and cinema, and along with 3D art, I'm drawn to the idea of creating pieces with unique styles and being able to convey them through a compelling story.

My portfolio covers a wide variety of projects, including websites, short films, animations or 3D models. Among my projects, my short film "Amanecer Pasado" stands out.
